## Risk of loss

Trading the financial markets carries a high level of risk and is not suitable
for everyone.

- You can lose some or all of the capital you invest.
- Leveraged products — futures, CFDs, options, forex, margin — can produce very
  rapid losses and, in some cases, losses exceeding your initial deposit.
- Volatility, market gaps, execution failures and technical problems can make a
  loss larger than expected.
- Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose, or with money needed for
  living costs, debt payments or emergencies.

## What we use today

At the time of writing, this website **sets no tracking, advertising or
analytics cookies**. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, and no
third-party tool following you between sites.

The only thing we store in your browser is your answer to the cookie notice:

| What is stored | Where | Why | How long |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `cookie-consent` | Your browser's local storage | To remember whether you accepted or declined, so we stop asking | Until you clear your browser data |

Technically this is local storage rather than a cookie, and it is strictly
necessary in order to respect your choice. It contains nothing that identifies
you and never leaves your device.

## What happens if you accept

Accepting the notice would allow us to set measurement cookies in future, to
learn which content is useful. **They are declined by default**: if you do
nothing, or if you press "Decline", none are set.

If a measurement tool is added later, this document will be updated before it is
switched on, naming the tool, what it collects and how long each cookie lasts.

## What is being sold

A live mentorship in a small group. It is not a recorded course and not a
platform of lessons to work through whenever you like.

Each month includes eight live sessions by video call, with a maximum of four
students per group, plus recordings of those sessions and access to the private
channel for questions between sessions. Times are agreed between the people in
the group and are set in US Eastern Time.

No trading signals are sold, no trading is carried out on anyone's behalf, and
no system or indicator promising results is supplied.

## How you are charged

The price is charged **monthly and recurring**, renewing automatically each
month until the student cancels. Payment is taken through Stripe using a
personal link sent after we talk. Nothing is charged on this website and no
card details are entered here at any point.

Every charge produces a receipt by email. That receipt contains a link the
student can use to cancel the subscription themselves at any time, without
asking permission or emailing anyone. The current month is the last one
charged; there are no further charges after that.

There is no minimum term and no cancellation penalty.

## Refund policy

There are no refunds for a month already begun. The sessions are delivered
live, the place in the group is taken, and it cannot be reassigned mid-month.

What does exist is the ability to stop at any time, which is what makes a
money-back guarantee unnecessary: nobody is required to pay in advance for a
period they have not used.

The [resources](/en/resources) page lists recommended books. Some of those links
are Amazon affiliate links: if you buy through one, Amazon pays a commission to
the operator of this website. The price you pay is exactly the same as going to
Amazon yourself.

The books on that list are there because the operator of this site read them and
recommends them. The commission does not decide what goes on the list, and some
titles on it carry no link at all.

Amazon sells and ships those books. This website is not part of the transaction:
returns, shipping and customer service are between you and Amazon.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
